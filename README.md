> # Career Confusion 📚

### Career Guidance & Major Recommendation System

**Career Confusion** is a web-based career guidance platform designed for students in Lebanon. It helps students discover suitable university majors and career paths based on their **interests, skills, personality, and career preferences**.

## ✨ Features

* 🧠 **Career Quiz** — Assess interests, skills, personality, and career preferences.
* 🎯 **Major Recommendations** — Receive personalized major matches and compatibility scores.
* 🎓 **Major & University Directory** — Explore 50+ majors and 30+ Lebanese universities.
* 🤖 **AI Career Assistant** — Get guidance about majors, careers, and academic paths.
* 👥 **Student Community** — Share questions, experiences, and advice.
* ⭐ **Saved Majors** — Save interesting majors for later.
* 📊 **Job-Market Charts** — Explore major demand and job-market information.
* 🛠️ **Admin Dashboard** — Manage users, majors, quiz questions, community content, and analytics.

## 🧠 Recommendation System

```text
Career Quiz
     ↓
Student Answers
     ↓
Score Calculation
     ↓
Major Matching
     ↓
Personalized Recommendations
```

**Database:** MySQL

**Tools:** XAMPP, Visual Studio Code
